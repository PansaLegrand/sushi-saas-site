screenshots. Names, reserved `.test` addresses, balances and job outcomes are
synthetic. No customer data, credentials, production jobs, revenue or adoption
metrics appear in the images. No invitations or provider jobs were sent.

These are read-only product previews, not an interactive SaaS deployment. The
site labels them as actual English screens with sample data in all five locales.
Capture the application again when its UI contract changes; do not draw features
into the screenshot or imply that the sample outcomes prove live-provider work.
Use the full-size links to inspect the original screenshot. The hero changes
